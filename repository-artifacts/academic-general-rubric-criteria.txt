ACE Academic General Rubric -- Criteria v1

This rubric is used for academic general evaluation under the Academic Consensus Engine (ACE) protocol.

Criterion criterion-1: Clarity and Coherence
Assess whether the submitted work presents ideas clearly and coherently. The argument must be well-structured, logical, and easy to follow.
Weight: 25 basis points. Minimum score: 0. Maximum score: 25.

Criterion criterion-2: Evidence and Support
Evaluate the quality and relevance of evidence provided in support of claims. Sources must be credible, properly cited, and directly relevant.
Weight: 25 basis points. Minimum score: 0. Maximum score: 25.

Criterion criterion-3: Depth of Analysis
Assess the depth and critical thinking demonstrated. Superficial summaries score low; insightful synthesis and original analysis score high.
Weight: 25 basis points. Minimum score: 0. Maximum score: 25.

Criterion criterion-4: Academic Integrity and Originality
Verify that the work represents the author's original contribution. Plagiarism, over-reliance on unattributed sources, or lack of original insight results in a failing score.
Weight: 25 basis points. Minimum score: 0. Maximum score: 25.

Scoring: Total score range 0-100. Passing threshold: 60. Required evaluator count: 3. Open review: false.
